# Tamilagaval — "First 30 Seconds" Template

Working playbook for the opening of every song upload. Created 2026-06-10 from
the channel's own YouTube Analytics retention data.

## Why this exists (the data)

Per-second audience-retention curves (% of viewers still watching):

| Into the video | முத்தமிழின் (54% template) | அந்தி மேகமே (typical, 19%) |
|---|---|---|
| ~18s | **82%** | 62% |
| **~36s** | **74%** | **46%** ← over half gone |
| ~85s | 59% | 20% |
| ~170s | 51% | 14% |

**The entire 54%-vs-19% gap is decided in the first ~30–36 seconds.** A typical
song loses ~40% of viewers in the first 18s and **more than half by 36s**, then
bleeds out. The template only loses 18% by 36s and plateaus near 50% for the
rest of the song. Tell: the template's opening reads **>100%** retention —
viewers scrubbed *back* to replay the hook.

**The one rule:** the opening isn't a "nice intro" — it's *deliver the emotional
payload before they can leave.*

## The opening blueprint (0–30s)

| Time | What plays | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **0–2s** | Voice or one strong instrument hook — never silence or a logo. First sound = the strongest line of the song (the *pallavi* hook). | Retention is highest at second 0 and only falls. A cold open on vocals is why முத்தமிழின் reads >100% (people rewind to re-hear it). |
| **2–8s** | The single most emotional line of the whole song, sung, with the Tamil lyric on screen (large, centered). | The "is this for me?" decision window. Lead with your best line, not the 3rd-best. |
| **8–20s** | Continue the hook / first charanam; keep the lyric on screen and the visual moving (cover art with subtle motion / relevant image — not a static frame). | Past 18s you've already beaten the average. Don't coast. |
| **20–30s** | Let it breathe into the verse — but only *after* the hook is banked. | By 30s you've either won (plateau ~50%) or lost (bleed to 20%). |

## The cardinal rule

**No long instrumental intro.** A 15–20s intro before the first word is the #1
reason the other songs drop to 46% by 36s. If the song needs an intro for the
full release, make the **YouTube cut start on the vocal** and move the intro
later — viewers won't wait.

## Two levers that feed the same loop

- **Thumbnail + title must match the hook.** CTR gets the click; the first 30s
  keeps them. Face/emotion + 3–4 large Tamil words beats a busy image. (Use the
  custom-thumbnail pipeline.)
- **Shorts as feeders:** cut the 0–15s hook into a vertical Short — the
  highest-retention slice by definition. Shorts feed = ~9% of channel traffic
  and sends people to the full song.

## Per-pillar opening move

- **Mother (அன்னை):** open on the most tender line, child-to-mother address —
  intimacy in line one.
- **Homeland (தாயகம்):** open on the image line (soil, sea, longing), not the
  abstract one.
- **Love (காதல்):** open on the sensory line — e.g. `கண்ணாலே நீர் அள்ளி…` —
  concrete, immediate, visual.

## Pre-publish checklist

- [ ] First sound is a voice/hook, not silence/intro/logo
- [ ] Strongest line is in the first 8 seconds
- [ ] Lyric on screen from ~second 2
- [ ] Visual moves in the first 30s
- [ ] A 15s hook Short is cut from the opening
- [ ] Thumbnail/title echo the opening line

## Companion levers (channel-level)

- **Upload cadence:** steady beats bursts. The cadence that matters is the one
  sustainable for 6 months — 1 song every 10–14 days you can keep > 1/week then
  silence (the burst-then-stop pattern is what caused the May 31 → Jun 6 dip).
- **Rotate the three pillars:** Mother / Homeland / Love.
- **Lyrics-as-asset (pending):** storing real lyrics feeds YouTube
  captions/descriptions AND searchable on-site lyrics pages — the ecosystem
  unlock. Blocked until lyrics are provided/stored.
